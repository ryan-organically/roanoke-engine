# Roanoke Coin: Decentralized Reserve Currency Specification

## Overview

Roanoke Coin evolves from a local trade token into the world's reserve currency through a **proof-of-sequestration** model. Rather than centralized minting or arbitrary scarcity, coins are generated through verifiable environmental labor—carbon capture and fuel synthesis—creating an economy where wealth generation directly reverses ecological damage.

---

## Core Philosophy

### Anti-Central Banking Model
- No central mint, no federal reserve equivalent
- Coin generation is **distributed to anyone performing sequestration work**
- Value is backed by measurable physical output (captured carbon, synthesized fuel)
- No inflation through arbitrary printing—supply tied to real environmental work

### The Climate-Currency Thesis
Traditional currencies decouple money from physical reality. Roanoke Coin re-couples them:
- **1 Roanoke Coin = 1 metric ton of verified CO2 sequestered OR 1 fuel block synthesized**
- Currency supply grows only as the atmosphere heals
- Economic growth = environmental restoration

## Carbon Sequestration Mechanics

### Sequestration Methods (Player-Operated)

#### 1. Biochar Kilns
- Convert organic matter (wood, crop waste, kelp) into stable carbon char
- Char must be buried or incorporated into soil to count
- Yield: Variable based on feedstock quality and kiln efficiency
- Verification: Soil carbon meters at burial sites

#### 2. Algae Cultivation Towers
- Vertical bioreactors growing carbon-hungry algae strains
- Algae harvested and compressed into carbon bricks
- Bricks stored in designated carbon vaults
- Higher tech, higher yield, requires coastal/water access

#### 3. Mechanical Carbon Scrubbers
- Late-game industrial technology
- Direct air capture using chemical sorbents
- Highest throughput but requires significant energy input
- Creates dependency on fuel block economy (circular system)

#### 4. Reforestation Bonds
- Plant trees, register them with the network
- Coins accrue slowly over tree lifetime (decades of passive income)
- Trees can be sold/traded with coin rights attached
- Cutting registered trees burns associated coins (accountability)

### The Verification Network

#### Distributed Sensor Mesh
- Players deploy verification nodes across the world
- Nodes measure: soil carbon, atmospheric CO2 locally, biomass density
- Running a node earns small coin fees from nearby sequestration claims
- Prevents fraudulent claims through triangulated measurement

#### Proof-of-Sequestration Protocol
1. Player performs carbon capture activity
2. Nearby sensor nodes detect and measure change
3. Measurement data broadcast to network
4. Consensus reached on carbon quantity
5. Coins minted to player's wallet proportional to capture

## Fuel Block Production

### The Fuel Block
A standardized energy unit synthesized from:
- Captured carbon (carbon source)
- Water (hydrogen source)
- Concentrated solar/geothermal energy (activation energy)

### Synthesis Process

#### Fischer-Tropsch Reactors
- Industrial facilities that combine CO2 + H2O + Energy → Hydrocarbon fuel blocks
- Endothermic process requiring significant energy input
- Output: Dense, stable, transportable fuel blocks
- Byproduct: Oxygen (valuable for enclosed habitats, underwater facilities)

#### Fuel Block Properties
- Energy density: Equivalent to diesel
- Shelf-stable indefinitely
- Burns clean (CO2 released was previously captured—net zero)
- Universal compatibility with engines, furnaces, generators

### The Fuel-Carbon Cycle
```
Atmosphere CO2 → Sequestration → Carbon Credits (Coins minted)
                                        ↓
Carbon Stock → Fuel Synthesis → Fuel Blocks (Coins burned as processing fee)
                                        ↓
Fuel Blocks → Combustion → Atmosphere CO2 (cycle continues)
```

The system is **net-negative** as long as more carbon is sequestered than synthesized into fuel.

## The Mechanical Internet Interface

### The Ledger Engines

Physical machines distributed across the world that maintain the Roanoke Coin network:

#### Architecture
- **No central server**—the "internet" is a mesh of player-built/maintained machines
- Each Ledger Engine stores a copy of all transactions
- Engines communicate via:
  - Telegraph lines (early game)
  - Radio relay towers (mid game)
  - Satellite uplinks (late game)

#### Running a Ledger Engine
- Requires: Mechanical computer components, power source, communication link
- Earns: Transaction fees from all network activity
- Risk: If your engine goes offline, you miss fees; extended downtime removes you from consensus

#### The Consensus Mechanism
- **Proof-of-Uptime**: Engines that stay online longest have more weight
- **Proof-of-Connectivity**: Engines with more peer connections have more weight
- Combined with Proof-of-Sequestration for coin minting
- No mining puzzles—useful work only

### Physical Network Topology

```
[Sequestration Site] ←→ [Sensor Nodes] ←→ [Local Ledger Engine]
                                                    ↓
                                            [Telegraph/Radio]
                                                    ↓
                                          [Regional Hub Engine]
                                                    ↓
                                            [Global Backbone]
```

Players can attack/disrupt the network physically:
- Cut telegraph lines
- Jam radio frequencies
- Destroy Ledger Engines

This creates emergent gameplay around protecting/attacking economic infrastructure.

## Reserve Currency Dynamics

### Why Nations Adopt Roanoke Coin

#### 1. Backed by Real Value
- Unlike fiat currencies, each coin represents verified environmental work
- Cannot be inflated away by political decisions
- Trustless—verification is mechanical, not institutional

#### 2. Aligned Incentives
- Nations that sequester more carbon have more money
- Wealth flows to environmental restoration, not extraction
- Economic competition becomes ecological competition

#### 3. Fuel Security
- Nations holding coins can always convert to fuel blocks
- Energy independence without fossil fuel dependency
- Fuel supply limited only by sequestration capacity

### The Transition Period

#### Phase 1: Parallel Currency
- Roanoke Coin circulates alongside existing currencies
- Exchange rates fluctuate based on sequestration rates
- Early adopters accumulate coins cheaply

#### Phase 2: Preferred Settlement
- International trade begins settling in Roanoke Coin
- Stability and backing make it preferred for large transactions
- Old currencies begin inflating as demand shifts

#### Phase 3: Reserve Status
- Central banks hold Roanoke Coin reserves
- Domestic currencies pegged to Coin
- Global economy now incentivized toward carbon negativity

## Solving Climate Change (Gameplay Narrative)

### The Tipping Point Mechanic

Global atmospheric CO2 tracked as world variable:
- Starts at dangerous levels (analogous to current ~420ppm)
- Every sequestration event reduces it slightly
- Every fuel burn increases it slightly
- **If players collectively achieve net-negative, world begins healing**

### Visible World Changes

As CO2 decreases:
- Extreme weather events become less frequent
- Coastal flooding recedes
- Coral reefs regenerate
- Agricultural yields improve
- New biomes become habitable

As CO2 increases:
- Opposite effects
- Economic pressure mounts (fuel becomes politically contentious)
- Players face social consequences for excessive burning

### The Win Condition

Reach pre-industrial CO2 levels (~280ppm):
- Climate stabilized
- Roanoke Coin becomes permanent reserve currency
- Players who contributed most to sequestration are immortalized
- World enters new era of sustainable prosperity

## Economic Gameplay Loops

### For Individual Players

**The Sequestration Farmer**
- Focus on biochar, algae, reforestation
- Steady coin income from environmental work
- Low risk, scales with land ownership

**The Fuel Magnate**
- Build fuel synthesis facilities
- Buy carbon credits, convert to fuel blocks
- Sell fuel to industry, transportation, military
- Higher margins but dependent on carbon market

**The Network Operator**
- Run Ledger Engines and sensor nodes
- Earn fees from all economic activity
- Critical infrastructure role
- PvP target

**The Carbon Speculator**
- Trade coin futures based on predicted sequestration rates
- Bet on weather, technology breakthroughs, political events
- Pure financial play

### For Factions/Nations

**The Green Economy**
- Maximize sequestration, export carbon credits
- Wealthy but potentially energy-poor if not balancing fuel production

**The Industrial Power**
- Heavy fuel consumption, must import credits or face deficit
- Military/manufacturing advantage but economic vulnerability

**The Balanced State**
- Self-sufficient in both carbon and fuel
- Resilient but slower growth

- Simplified mesh network topology

## Open Design Questions

1. **Deflation Risk**: If sequestration outpaces fuel demand, coins become increasingly valuable—good for holders, bad for commerce. Demurrage (holding fees)?

2. **Bootstrapping**: How do players get initial coins before sequestration infrastructure exists? Founding grants? Labor vouchers?

3. **Cheating Prevention**: What stops players from falsifying sensor data? Physical inspection mechanics? Reputation systems?

4. **International Enforcement**: If a nation refuses to honor coin obligations, what recourse exists? Trade embargoes? Military intervention?

5. **Technology Asymmetry**: Advanced players can sequester far more efficiently—does this create problematic wealth concentration?

## Conclusion

Roanoke Coin as a reserve currency creates a world where:
- **Economic success requires environmental restoration**
- **The network is physical infrastructure players build and defend**
- **Climate change is a solvable collective action problem with clear incentives**
- **Fuel abundance comes from healing the atmosphere, not depleting it**

The currency becomes the mechanism by which the world saves itself—not through regulation or sacrifice, but through aligned incentives where doing well and doing good are the same thing.
