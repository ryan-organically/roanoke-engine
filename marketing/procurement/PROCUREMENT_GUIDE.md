## 1. Purpose

This guide establishes standards and processes for procuring goods, services, and partnerships for Roanoke Interactive, Inc. It ensures we work with vendors who share our values, meet our quality standards, and provide fair terms.

---

## 2. Procurement Principles

### 2.1 Core Values

**Quality First**
We prioritize quality over lowest cost. Cheap rarely means good, and our players deserve the best.

**Fair Dealing**
We pay fair prices, honor commitments, and treat vendors as partners.

**Sustainability**
We prefer vendors with sustainable practices and ethical operations.

**Diversity**
We actively seek diverse suppliers and partners.

**Transparency**
We're clear about our needs, timelines, and decision criteria.

### 2.2 Ethical Standards

All vendors must:
- Comply with applicable laws
- Maintain ethical labor practices
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Protect confidential information
- Not engage in bribery or corruption

## 4. Vendor Evaluation

### 4.1 Evaluation Criteria

All vendors evaluated on:

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Quality | 30% | Output/service quality |
| Reliability | 20% | Delivery, uptime, consistency |
| Price | 15% | Cost-effectiveness (not lowest) |
| Values Fit | 15% | Alignment with our principles |
| Capability | 10% | Technical/creative ability |
| Relationship | 10% | Communication, flexibility |

### 4.2 Evaluation Process

**Tier 1: Small Purchases (< $10K)**
- Single approver (department lead)
- Vendor checklist completion
- No formal RFP required

**Tier 2: Medium Purchases ($10K - $100K)**
- Department lead + Finance approval
- Minimum 2 vendor comparison
- Reference checks
- Contract review

**Tier 3: Large Purchases ($100K - $500K)**
- Executive approval required
- Formal RFP process
- Minimum 3 vendor comparison
- Detailed evaluation scorecard
- Legal contract review

**Tier 4: Strategic Purchases (> $500K)**
- CEO/Board approval
- Full RFP with evaluation committee
- Due diligence process
- Negotiated terms
- Executive contract execution

### 4.3 Due Diligence

**Standard Due Diligence (All Vendors):**
- Business license verification
- Reference checks (2+)
- Online reputation review
- Basic financial stability

**Enhanced Due Diligence (Tier 3+):**
- Financial statement review
- Legal standing verification
- Insurance verification
- Security assessment (tech vendors)
- Compliance certification review

**Strategic Due Diligence (Tier 4):**
- Third-party financial analysis
- Management interviews
- Site visits (where applicable)
- Long-term viability assessment
- Contingency planning

## 5. Contract Standards

### 5.1 Required Terms

All vendor contracts must include:

**Standard Clauses:**
- Scope of work / deliverables
- Payment terms
- Timeline / milestones
- Acceptance criteria
- Warranty / quality standards
- Confidentiality
- Intellectual property assignment
- Termination rights
- Limitation of liability
- Governing law / dispute resolution

**Technology Vendor Additions:**
- SLA / uptime guarantees
- Data security requirements
- Privacy compliance
- Audit rights
- Disaster recovery
- Exit assistance

**Creative Vendor Additions:**
- IP assignment (work for hire)
- Rights scope (platforms, territories)
- Revision limits
- Credit/attribution
- Portfolio rights

### 5.2 Terms to Avoid

**Red Flags:**
- Automatic renewal without notice
- Price increase without cap
- Unlimited liability (for us)
- Exclusive vendor lock-in
- Unreasonable IP claims
- Non-negotiable "standard" terms

### 5.3 Contract Templates

Maintained templates:
- Software License Agreement
- Services Agreement
- Contractor Agreement (individual)
- NDA (one-way, mutual)
- Work-for-Hire Agreement
- Content License Agreement

All contracts require Legal review before execution.

## 6. Vendor Management

### 6.1 Onboarding

**New Vendor Checklist:**
- [ ] Contract executed
- [ ] W-9 / payment info collected
- [ ] Vendor portal account created
- [ ] NDA executed (if applicable)
- [ ] Primary contact established
- [ ] Kickoff meeting scheduled
- [ ] Success criteria defined

### 6.2 Performance Monitoring

**Quarterly Review:**
- Deliverable quality assessment
- SLA compliance (tech vendors)
- Budget adherence
- Relationship health
- Issues/escalations log

**Annual Review:**
- Full performance scorecard
- Renewal recommendation
- Pricing review
- Scope adjustment needs

### 6.3 Issue Management

**Escalation Path:**
1. Vendor account manager
2. Vendor leadership
3. Our procurement lead
4. Our executive sponsor
5. Contract remedies

**Remedies:**
- Service credits
- Reperformance
- Price reduction
- Contract modification
- Termination

## 7. Sustainability & Ethics

### 7.1 Sustainability Preferences

We prefer vendors who:
- Use renewable energy
- Minimize waste
- Source materials responsibly
- Offset carbon footprint
- Report on sustainability metrics

**Merchandise Vendors Must:**
- Use sustainable materials (no single-use plastic)
- Ethical manufacturing (no sweatshops)
- Provide supply chain transparency
- Meet safety/quality standards

### 7.2 Diversity & Inclusion

**Goals:**
- 25% of addressable spend to diverse suppliers
- Track and report diversity metrics
- Active outreach to diverse vendors
- Mentorship for small/diverse suppliers

**Diverse Supplier Definition:**
- Minority-owned
- Women-owned
- Veteran-owned
- LGBTQ+-owned
- Disability-owned
- Small business

### 7.3 Ethical Requirements

**Zero Tolerance:**
- Child labor
- Forced labor
- Bribery / corruption
- Environmental destruction
- Discrimination

**Verification:**
- Supplier questionnaire
- Third-party audits (high-risk)
- Whistleblower channels
- Annual certification

## 8. Procurement Operations

### 8.1 Purchase Authorization

| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| < $1,000 | Manager |
| $1,000 - $10,000 | Director |
| $10,000 - $50,000 | VP |
| $50,000 - $250,000 | C-Level |
| > $250,000 | CEO + Board notification |

### 8.2 Payment Terms

**Standard:** Net 30

**Exceptions:**
- Contractors: Upon completion or bi-weekly
- Large projects: Milestone-based
- Subscriptions: Annual (preferred for discount)
- International: May vary by region

**Early Payment:**
- Offered for 2%+ discount
- Case-by-case approval

### 8.3 Budget Management

**Planning:**
- Annual vendor budget by department
- Quarterly review and adjustment
- Contingency reserve (10%)

**Tracking:**
- All purchases against budget
- Monthly variance reporting
- Quarterly reforecast
