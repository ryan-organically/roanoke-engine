**Abstract:** This whitepaper establishes the governance framework for the Roanoke platform, defining policies for content moderation, user rights, dispute resolution, and community standards. We detail our commitment to transparency, fairness, and user empowerment while maintaining platform safety and integrity.

---

## 1. Governance Philosophy

### 1.1 Core Principles

**1. Transparency**
Users have the right to understand the rules they're subject to and how decisions are made.

**2. Fairness**
Rules apply equally to all users. Enforcement is consistent and proportionate.

**3. Due Process**
Users have the right to know why actions are taken and to appeal decisions.

**4. User Empowerment**
Where possible, users should have control over their experience.

**5. Minimum Necessary Intervention**
We intervene only when necessary to protect users, the platform, or legal compliance.

### 1.2 Governance Scope

This framework governs:
- Roanoke game servers (official)
- Roanoke Marketplace
- Roanoke Engine usage
- Official community platforms (Discord, forums)
- User-generated content
- Creator and developer programs

## 2. Content Standards

### 2.1 Universal Standards

Content that is **always prohibited**:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Illegal Content | CSAM, terrorism, incitement to violence |
| Harassment | Targeted attacks, doxxing, stalking |
| Hate Speech | Discrimination based on protected characteristics |
| Fraud | Scams, deceptive practices, impersonation |
| Privacy Violations | Non-consensual sharing of personal information |
| IP Violations | Copyright/trademark infringement |
| Platform Manipulation | Exploits, hacks, bots that harm others |

### 2.2 Context-Dependent Standards

Content moderated based on context:

| Content Type | Consideration |
|--------------|---------------|
| Violence | Game-appropriate vs. gratuitous/realistic |
| Adult Themes | Age-gated communities vs. general access |
| Controversial Topics | Discussion vs. harassment |
| Competitive Behavior | Fair play vs. exploitation |
| Commercial Activity | Authorized vs. unauthorized |

### 2.3 Age-Appropriate Content

**Rating System:**
| Rating | Age | Content |
|--------|-----|---------|
| Everyone | All ages | No objectionable content |
| Teen | 13+ | Mild violence, themes |
| Mature | 18+ | Strong themes, violence |
| Adult | 18+ (verified) | Explicit content (separate platform) |

**Default Settings:**
- New accounts default to Teen-appropriate content
- Age verification required for Mature/Adult
- Parental controls available

## 3. User Rights

### 3.1 Fundamental Rights

All Roanoke users are entitled to:

**1. Privacy**
- Control over personal data
- Transparent data practices
- Right to deletion (where legally permissible)
- No sale of personal data

**2. Expression**
- Create and share content within guidelines
- Express opinions and perspectives
- Participate in community discussions

**3. Ownership**
- Own creations (with platform license)
- Export personal data
- Transfer/sell owned items (where enabled)

**4. Fair Treatment**
- Equal rule application
- Explanation of enforcement actions
- Right to appeal

**5. Safety**
- Tools to protect from harassment
- Reporting mechanisms
- Block/mute capabilities

### 3.2 Creator Rights

Creators (modders, content creators, developers) have additional rights:

**Revenue:**
- 88% of Marketplace sales (12% platform fee)
- Clear, timely payment
- Transparent sales reporting

**Attribution:**
- Credit for creations
- Protection against unauthorized copying
- Trademark on established brands

**Independence:**
- Right to publish elsewhere
- Export created content
- End partnership with reasonable notice

### 3.3 Developer Rights

Developers using Roanoke Engine/Marketplace:

**Business Terms:**
- Clear, stable licensing terms
- 30-day notice of material changes
- Grandfathering for existing agreements

**Technical Access:**
- API stability commitments
- Deprecation notices
- Migration assistance

**Data:**
- Access to own game analytics
- Player data (with consent)
- Revenue reporting

## 4. Enforcement System

### 4.1 Enforcement Principles

**Graduated Response:**
Most violations escalate through levels before permanent action.

**Proportionality:**
Punishment fits the violation severity.

**Rehabilitation:**
Focus on education and behavior change, not punishment alone.

**Consistency:**
Same violations receive same treatment across users.

### 4.2 Enforcement Actions

| Level | Action | Duration | Applies To |
|-------|--------|----------|------------|
| 0 | Warning | N/A | First minor violations |
| 1 | Temporary Restriction | 24 hours | Repeated minor violations |
| 2 | Extended Restriction | 7 days | Moderate violations |
| 3 | Suspension | 30 days | Serious violations |
| 4 | Permanent Ban | Indefinite | Severe/repeated serious |

**Restrictions may include:**
- Chat mute
- Matchmaking restriction
- Feature restrictions
- Marketplace suspension
- Full account suspension

### 4.3 Violation Categories

**Minor Violations:**
- Mild rudeness
- Minor spam
- Off-topic posting
- Accidental guideline breach

*Typical Response:* Warning, education

**Moderate Violations:**
- Repeated minor violations
- Targeted negativity
- Minor exploits (non-harmful)
- Misleading content

*Typical Response:* Temporary restriction

**Serious Violations:**
- Harassment
- Discriminatory content
- Malicious exploits
- Fraud attempts
- Repeated moderate violations

*Typical Response:* Suspension

**Severe Violations:**
- Illegal content
- Major harassment campaigns
- Platform attacks
- Repeated serious violations

*Typical Response:* Permanent ban

### 4.4 Detection Methods

**Automated Systems:**
- Content filtering (text, image)
- Behavior pattern detection
- Spam/bot detection
- Cheat detection

**Human Review:**
- Reported content review
- Escalated automated flags
- Appeal reviews
- Proactive moderation

**Community:**
- User reporting
- Trusted flaggers program
- Community moderators (Discord, forums)

## 5. Appeals Process

### 5.1 Appeal Rights

Users may appeal:
- Any enforcement action Level 1 or above
- Content removal decisions
- Account restrictions
- Marketplace decisions

Users may NOT appeal:
- Warnings (no actionable consequence)
- Automated temporary holds (pending review)
- Legal compliance actions

### 5.2 Appeal Process

**Step 1: Initial Appeal**
- Submit via appeal form within 14 days
- Include: Action ID, explanation, evidence
- Response within 7 business days

**Step 2: First Review**
- Different moderator from original action
- Full context review
- Uphold, modify, or overturn

**Step 3: Escalated Appeal (if denied)**
- Submit within 7 days of first review
- Senior moderator or policy team review
- Response within 14 business days
- Decision is final for most cases

**Step 4: External Review (severe cases only)**
- Available for permanent bans only
- Independent review panel
- Limited to policy interpretation, not facts
- 30-day review period

### 5.3 Appeal Outcomes

| Outcome | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Overturned | Action removed, record expunged |
| Modified | Action reduced, record updated |
| Upheld | Original action stands |
| Escalated | Sent to higher review |

## 6. Dispute Resolution

### 6.1 User-to-User Disputes

**In-Game Disputes:**
- Use block/mute tools
- Report if guidelines violated
- Moderator intervention if needed

**Trade/Commercial Disputes:**
- Document transactions
- Submit support ticket
- Mediation available
- Binding arbitration (Marketplace terms)

**Creative Disputes:**
- Copyright/trademark: DMCA process
- Attribution disputes: Mediation
- Revenue disputes: Platform arbitration

### 6.2 User-to-Platform Disputes

**Support Escalation:**
1. Support ticket
2. Supervisor review
3. Director review
4. Executive escalation (rare)

**Formal Dispute Resolution:**
- Per Terms of Service
- Binding arbitration (most disputes)
- Small claims court option
- Class action waiver (where enforceable)

### 6.3 Developer/Creator Disputes

**Commercial Disputes:**
- Contract terms govern
- Business development mediation
- Formal arbitration if needed

**Technical Disputes:**
- Support escalation
- Technical review board
- Service credits/remediation

## 8. Community Input

### 8.1 Feedback Channels

**Ongoing:**
- Feedback forums
- In-game surveys
- Support ticket trends
- Social listening

**Periodic:**
- Community advisory council
- Policy RFC process
- Public comment periods
- User research studies

### 8.2 Policy Development

**Significant Policy Changes:**
1. Internal proposal
2. Legal/compliance review
3. Community RFC (14-day comment)
4. Revision based on feedback
5. Announcement (14-day notice)
6. Implementation

**Minor Policy Updates:**
1. Internal review
2. Announcement
3. Immediate or short-notice implementation

### 8.3 Community Advisory Council

**Composition:**
- 7-11 members
- Diverse representation (players, creators, developers)
- Geographic diversity
- 1-year terms, renewable
- Volunteer (stipend for meetings)

**Role:**
- Advise on policy development
- Represent community perspectives
- Review major decisions
- No binding authority, advisory only

## 9. Compliance & Legal

### 9.1 Regulatory Compliance

**Global:**
- GDPR (EU data protection)
- DSA (EU Digital Services Act)
- Age verification laws
- Copyright (DMCA, EU Copyright Directive)

**Regional:**
- Country-specific content laws
- Local consumer protection
- Tax and reporting requirements

### 9.2 Law Enforcement

**Request Process:**
1. Valid legal process required
2. Legal review of request
3. Narrow scope compliance
4. User notification (where permitted)
5. Transparency reporting

**We Will:**
- Comply with valid legal process
- Protect user rights within law
- Challenge overbroad requests
- Notify users when allowed

**We Will Not:**
- Provide data without legal process
- Build surveillance capabilities
- Assist with political persecution
- Keep compliance secret (unless legally required)

### 9.3 Content Liability

**Platform Position:**
- We're a platform, not publisher
- Section 230 (US) protections claimed
- Good faith moderation
- Notice-and-takedown for IP

**Creator/Developer Responsibility:**
- Creators responsible for their content
- Indemnification in creator terms
- Platform reserves right to remove

## 10. Platform Safety

### 10.1 Child Safety

**Age Requirements:**
- Minimum age: 13 (or local equivalent)
- Parental consent under 16 (where required)
- Age verification for mature content

**Protections:**
- No direct messaging with unknown adults (minors)
- Restricted content filtering
- Parental controls available
- Enhanced monitoring

**Reporting:**
- NCMEC reporting for CSAM
- Priority review for child safety
- Cooperation with law enforcement

### 10.2 Anti-Exploitation

**Prevention:**
- Content scanning
- Behavior pattern detection
- Trusted flagger program
- Proactive investigation

**Response:**
- Immediate removal
- Law enforcement referral
- Account termination
- Support resources for victims

### 10.3 Crisis Response

**Imminent Harm:**
- 24/7 escalation path
- Law enforcement coordination
- Emergency contact protocols
- Crisis support resources

**Platform Crises:**
- Incident response team
- Communication plan
- Post-incident review
- Public reporting
