## 1. Philosophy

### 1.1 Community-First Principle

> "The community is not a marketing channel. The community IS the product."

Traditional games treat community as an afterthought—a support function or marketing tool. Roanoke inverts this: community health is a core product metric, equal to technical performance or content quality.

### 1.2 The Citizen Model

We don't want "users" or "customers." We want **citizens**—invested participants who:

- Feel ownership over their experience
- Contribute to community culture
- Help newcomers find their footing
- Advocate authentically without prompting
- Provide honest feedback, including criticism
- Identify with Roanoke as part of their identity

**Progression:**

```
Discoverer → Player → Member → Contributor → Advocate → Citizen
```

## 3. Onboarding Journey

### 3.1 First-Time Player Experience

**Day 0: Discovery**
- Source: Meme, friend, influencer, ad
- Action: Visit website or Steam page
- Emotion: Curiosity, intrigue

*Optimization:*
- Clear value proposition
- Immediate emotional hook
- Easy path to action

**Day 0: Decision**
- Action: Purchase or wishlist
- Emotion: Anticipation, minor risk

*Optimization:*
- Demo/free trial option
- Strong social proof (reviews)
- Clear refund policy

**Day 1: First Session**
- Action: Tutorial, initial exploration
- Emotion: Wonder, slight overwhelm

*Optimization:*
- Tutorialization that respects intelligence
- Early "wow" moments
- Clear but unforced objectives

**Day 1-7: Learning**
- Action: Mechanics mastery, first builds
- Emotion: Growing competence, occasional frustration

*Optimization:*
- Responsive difficulty
- Clear progress indicators
- Community resources easily discoverable

**Day 7-30: Integration**
- Action: Join community, make friends
- Emotion: Belonging, investment

*Optimization:*
- In-game guild discovery
- Discord integration
- Social prompts (non-invasive)

### 3.2 Discord Onboarding

**Welcome Flow:**

1. **Arrival Message** (DM from bot):
```
Welcome to the Colony, {username}! 🌲

You've joined [X] other settlers in the Roanoke community.

Here's how to get started:
• Read the #welcome channel for community guidelines
• Introduce yourself in #introductions
• Ask questions in #tips-and-tricks
• Find a group in #looking-for-group

Questions? Our community helpers (🏷️ Pathfinders) are here to help.

What happened at Roanoke? Let's find out together.
```

2. **Role Selection** (reaction roles):
- Platform (PC, Console, Future)
- Play style (Solo, Coop, Competitive)
- Interests (Building, Combat, Lore, Modding)
- Content creator (Y/N)

3. **Introduction Prompt**:
```
Share a bit about yourself:
• How did you discover Roanoke?
• What are you most excited about?
• Any questions for the community?
```

### 3.3 Mentor Program

**Pathfinder System:**

Experienced players volunteer to help newcomers.

*Pathfinder Requirements:*
- 100+ hours playtime
- Active Discord presence (3+ months)
- No moderation issues
- Completed training module
- Community vouches (2+)

*Pathfinder Responsibilities:*
- Monitor #support and #tips-and-tricks
- Welcome new members
- Host "orientation sessions" weekly
- Escalate issues to staff
- Model community values

*Pathfinder Benefits:*
- Exclusive Pathfinder role
- In-game cosmetic (special cape/badge)
- Early access to beta content
- Direct line to community team
- Annual Pathfinder appreciation event

- Typing "CROATOAN" in chat when something weird happens
- Building memorial stones for perished characters
- New player "welcome fires" in-game
- Community moments of silence for major updates

### 5.2 Values Transmission

**Core Community Values:**

1. **Welcome the Lost**
   - Every newcomer was once lost; help them find their way
   - No question is too basic
   - Patience with learning curves

2. **Discover Together**
   - Share discoveries, don't hoard
   - Spoiler warnings are sacred
   - Theories are for discussing, not gatekeeping

3. **Build, Don't Burn**
   - Constructive criticism over complaints
   - Create content, not drama
   - Conflicts resolved privately

4. **Respect the Mystery**
   - Some things are meant to be unknown
   - The journey matters as much as the destination
   - Wonder is valuable

**Value Reinforcement:**
- Moderators model values
- Community awards for value demonstration
- Stories featuring value examples
- Gentle correction (not punishment) for violations

### 5.3 Toxicity Prevention

**Proactive Measures:**

1. **Norm Setting:**
   - Clear guidelines (not rules)
   - Positive framing ("we do X" not "don't do Y")
   - Staff/moderator modeling

2. **Community Immune System:**
   - Empower members to self-moderate
   - Upvote/downvote systems
   - Reporting tools (anonymous, easy)

3. **Structural Design:**
   - Cool-off periods for heated threads
   - Topic channels to prevent cross-contamination
   - Slow mode for large announcements

**Reactive Measures:**

| Behavior | First | Second | Third |
|----------|-------|--------|-------|
| Minor rudeness | Gentle reminder (public) | Warning (DM) | Timeout (1h) |
| Harassment | Warning (DM) | Timeout (24h) | Ban |
| Hate speech | Immediate ban | - | - |
| Spam | Timeout (1h) | Ban | - |
| Doxing/threats | Immediate ban + report | - | - |

## 6. Measurement Framework

### 6.1 Community Health Metrics

**Engagement Metrics:**

| Metric | Definition | Target | Frequency |
|--------|------------|--------|-----------|
| DAU/MAU (Discord) | Daily / Monthly active | 0.25 | Daily |
| Messages/DAU | Chat activity | 5+ | Daily |
| Threads created | Discussion initiation | 20/day | Daily |
| Response time | Staff reply speed | <4h | Daily |
| First-time posters | New voices | 50/day | Daily |

**Sentiment Metrics:**

| Metric | Definition | Target | Frequency |
|--------|------------|--------|-----------|
| NPS | Net Promoter Score | 70+ | Monthly |
| Sentiment ratio | Positive/Negative posts | 5:1 | Weekly |
| Support satisfaction | Ticket resolution rating | 4.5/5 | Per ticket |
| Steam review trend | 30-day review score | 90%+ | Weekly |

**Health Metrics:**

| Metric | Definition | Target | Frequency |
|--------|------------|--------|-----------|
| Moderation actions | Bans, timeouts | Declining | Weekly |
| Report rate | Reports / 1000 messages | <5 | Weekly |
| Churn (Discord) | Left server / total | <5%/mo | Monthly |
| Toxicity score | ML-detected negativity | <2% | Daily |

## 8. Scaling Considerations

### 8.1 Growth Stages

**Stage 1: Founding (0 - 10K members)**
- Team: 1-2 community managers
- Focus: Culture establishment, core community
- Automation: Minimal
- Personal touch: Maximum

**Stage 2: Growth (10K - 100K members)**
- Team: 3-5 community managers + volunteers
- Focus: Systems, onboarding, programs
- Automation: Onboarding, moderation assist
- Personal touch: Targeted (creators, issues)

**Stage 3: Scale (100K - 1M members)**
- Team: 10+ community professionals + large volunteer org
- Focus: Self-sustaining community, regional expansion
- Automation: Significant (AI moderation, routing)
- Personal touch: Strategic (events, VIPs)

**Stage 4: Institution (1M+ members)**
- Team: Full department (20+)
- Focus: Governance, sub-communities, global
- Automation: AI-first, human oversight
- Personal touch: Leadership visibility, milestone events

Community is Roanoke's competitive advantage that cannot be purchased or replicated. A thriving community creates value for every player through content, support, social connection, and cultural meaning.

The frameworks in this document guide us from deliberate community building in early stages to nurturing a self-sustaining culture as we scale. Our role evolves from builder to gardener to steward—always serving the community we've been trusted to host.
