## 10. RISK FACTORS

### 10.1 Market Risks

**Competition:** Major platform companies (Epic, Valve, Microsoft) have significantly more resources and could compete directly or acquire competitors.

*Mitigation:* Community-first approach creates loyalty difficult for large companies to replicate; full-stack integration provides unique value proposition.

**Market Contraction:** Gaming industry could face downturn due to economic conditions or shifting consumer preferences.

*Mitigation:* Gaming historically recession-resistant; Roanoke's engaged community provides stability; multiple revenue streams reduce dependence.

### 10.2 Technology Risks

**Technical Debt:** Rapid development may create scalability or maintenance challenges.

*Mitigation:* Ongoing refactoring prioritized; experienced engineering leadership; code review processes.

**Security:** Gaming platforms are targets for hackers and bad actors.

*Mitigation:* Security-first architecture; third-party audits; bug bounty program.

### 10.3 Execution Risks

**Key Person Dependency:** Company depends heavily on founding team.

*Mitigation:* Senior hires diversifying leadership; documentation of institutional knowledge; equity retention programs.

**Scaling Challenges:** Moving from product to platform requires different skills and processes.

*Mitigation:* Hiring experienced platform operators; advisory board guidance; phased rollout.

### 10.4 Regulatory Risks

**Platform Regulation:** Increasing scrutiny of digital platforms and app stores.

*Mitigation:* Developer-friendly terms preempt regulatory concerns; proactive compliance posture.

**Content Moderation:** User-generated content creates liability exposure.

*Mitigation:* Robust moderation tools; clear community guidelines; responsive trust & safety team.
