## Game Description

### One-Line
> An open-world survival game set in the mystery of America's Lost Colony.

### Short Description (100 words)
Roanoke drops you into the wilderness of 1587 Roanoke Island, where 117 colonists vanished without a trace. Build, survive, and uncover the truth through exploration, crafting, and emergent storytelling. The procedurally-generated world ensures no two games are alike, while persistent multiplayer servers let you build settlements, form alliances, and write new history with thousands of other players. Piece together the mystery through environmental clues, discoverable artifacts, and lore that the community unravels together.

What happened at Roanoke? Find out for yourself.

### Full Description (300 words)
In 1590, John White returned to Roanoke Island to find his colony abandoned. Over 100 men, women, and children had vanished. The only clue: the word "CROATOAN" carved into a tree. It remains America's oldest unsolved mystery.

Roanoke reimagines this historical enigma as an immersive survival experience. You'll wake on unfamiliar shores with nothing but the clothes on your back and a burning question: what happened here?

**Survive the Wilderness**
Hunt deer, gather berries, craft tools, and build shelter against the elements. The Outer Banks are beautiful but unforgiving—manage hunger, thirst, temperature, and stamina as you establish your foothold in the New World. Dynamic weather, day/night cycles, and seasonal changes ensure the land itself is a character in your story.

**Build Your Legacy**
From crude lean-tos to sprawling fortified settlements, construction is limited only by imagination. Our freeform building system supports structural physics, defensive works, and aesthetic customization. Your creations persist in the world, visible to future players and etched into the landscape.

**Uncover the Mystery**
The colonial mystery isn't a linear narrative—it's a puzzle scattered across the world. Environmental storytelling, discoverable documents, and artifact examination gradually reveal what befell the original settlers. Some mysteries take the entire community months to solve. Others may never be fully understood.

**Join the Colony**
Play solo for an introspective survival experience, co-op with friends, or join persistent servers with thousands of players. Form guilds, establish trade routes, wage wars, or simply exist in a living world shaped by human choices.

Powered by our proprietary Roanoke Engine, featuring advanced procedural generation, AI-driven wildlife, and seamless multiplayer at unprecedented scale.

The colony disappeared in 1590. Build a new one that lasts forever.

## Key Features

- **Procedurally Generated World:** Infinite, unique wilderness with historically-accurate flora, fauna, and geography
- **Deep Survival Systems:** Realistic hunger, thirst, temperature, injury, and stamina management
- **Freeform Building:** Construct anything from campfires to castles with structural physics
- **Emergent Mystery:** Piece together the Lost Colony's fate through exploration and discovery
- **Living AI Ecosystem:** Wildlife with pack behaviors, migration patterns, and predator-prey dynamics
- **Persistent Multiplayer:** Thousands of concurrent players on servers that never reset
- **Modding Support:** Full mod tools and creator marketplace with revenue sharing
- **Cross-Platform Progression:** Play on any platform, keep your character

## Press FAQ

**Q: Is Roanoke a horror game?**
A: Roanoke is primarily a survival game with mystery elements. While it can be tense and atmospheric, it's not designed as horror. The mystery is about discovery, not scares.

**Q: Is the game historically accurate?**
A: We've researched extensively to create an authentic environment, but we take creative liberties for gameplay. Think "historically inspired" rather than simulation.

**Q: Can I play solo?**
A: Absolutely. Roanoke offers a complete single-player experience. Multiplayer is optional.

**Q: Is there PvP?**
A: PvP exists in designated zones on multiplayer servers. Servers can enable/disable PvP globally.

**Q: Is there a narrative campaign?**
A: The mystery unfolds through exploration and discovery rather than a linear narrative. The community collectively pieces together the story.

**Q: What makes Roanoke different from other survival games?**
A: The historical mystery as core motivator, the procedural lore system, the scale of multiplayer, and the community-driven discovery model.
