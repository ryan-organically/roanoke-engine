## Document Lifecycle

### Creation
1. Author drafts document
2. Peer review
3. Department head approval
4. Legal review (if external)
5. Classification assignment
6. Index addition

### Updates
1. Quarterly review cycle
2. Owner responsible for currency
3. Version increment for material changes
4. Changelog maintenance

### Retirement
1. Mark as deprecated
2. Redirect to replacement
3. Archive after 1 year
4. Remove from index

## Whitepaper Series Summary

### Technical Series (WP-001 to WP-009)
Deep technical documentation on engine capabilities, architecture, and implementation details. Primarily for developers, researchers, and technical press.

### Business Series (WP-010 to WP-019)
Strategic and business analysis documents covering economics, market positioning, and growth strategy. Primarily for internal leadership and investors.

### Community Series (WP-020 to WP-029)
Community management, creator programs, and social strategy documentation. Primarily for community team and partners.

### Governance Series (WP-030 to WP-039)
Platform policies, legal frameworks, and compliance documentation. For users, legal, and regulatory purposes.
