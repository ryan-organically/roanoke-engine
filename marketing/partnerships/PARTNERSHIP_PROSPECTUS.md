## 3. Partnership Process

### 3.1 Evaluation Criteria

All partnerships are evaluated against:

1. **Authenticity Fit**
   - Does this align with Roanoke's brand and values?
   - Will our community appreciate this?
   - Is there natural synergy?

2. **Player Value**
   - Does this create value for players?
   - Does this enhance or detract from gameplay?
   - Will players thank us or complain?

3. **Business Sustainability**
   - Does this make financial sense for both parties?
   - Is this a one-off or ongoing relationship?
   - What's the long-term potential?

4. **Execution Feasibility**
   - Can we deliver this well?
   - Do we have bandwidth and resources?
   - What's the timeline?

### 3.2 Engagement Process

**Step 1: Initial Inquiry**
- Submit inquiry via partnerships@playroanoke.com
- Include: Company overview, partnership idea, goals
- Response within 5 business days

**Step 2: Discovery Call**
- 30-minute introductory call
- Align on potential scope
- Identify decision-makers

**Step 3: Proposal Development**
- Either party develops detailed proposal
- Creative concepts, terms, timeline
- Internal review

**Step 4: Negotiation**
- Terms refinement
- Legal review
- Contract development

**Step 5: Approval & Execution**
- Final approval from Roanoke leadership
- Contract execution
- Project kickoff
