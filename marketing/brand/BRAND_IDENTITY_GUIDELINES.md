# ROANOKE Brand Identity Guidelines
## Version 1.0 | Institutional Document

---

## Executive Summary

This document establishes the comprehensive brand identity system for Roanoke, governing all visual, verbal, and experiential expressions of the brand across all platforms, media, and touchpoints. Adherence to these guidelines ensures brand consistency, recognition, and equity preservation as Roanoke scales to a global entertainment platform.

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

### 1.1 Brand Essence

**Core Identity Statement:**
> Roanoke is the threshold between the known and the unknown—a place where mystery becomes discovery, where solitude becomes community, and where the lost become the found.

**Brand Promise:**
> Every player who enters Roanoke discovers not just a world, but themselves.

**Brand Position:**
> The definitive survival experience that combines historical mystery, emergent storytelling, and authentic community in a living, breathing world.

### 1.2 Brand Pillars

| Pillar | Definition | Expression |
|--------|------------|------------|
| **Mystery** | The unknown drives exploration | Hidden lore, discoverable secrets, emergent narratives |
| **Survival** | Meaningful challenge and growth | Fair difficulty, skill progression, earned mastery |
| **Community** | Shared experience and belonging | Guilds, events, collaborative building, inside jokes |
| **Discovery** | Constant revelation and wonder | Procedural generation, updates, player-created content |
| **Legacy** | Permanent impact and memory | Persistent world, player monuments, historical record |

### 1.3 Brand Personality

**Primary Traits:**
- **Enigmatic**: We reveal gradually, never completely
- **Authentic**: We are genuine, never performative
- **Inviting**: We welcome all, exclude none
- **Deep**: Surface simplicity conceals profound complexity
- **Resilient**: We endure, adapt, and thrive

**Voice Characteristics:**
- Confident but not arrogant
- Mysterious but not pretentious
- Warm but not saccharine
- Knowledgeable but not condescending
- Playful but not juvenile

## 2. Visual Identity

### 2.1 Logo System

**Primary Logo: The Croatoan Mark**

The primary logo consists of the wordmark "ROANOKE" with the integrated Croatoan symbol—a stylized tree carving representing the historical mystery.

**Logo Variations:**
| Variant | Use Case | Minimum Size |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| Full Horizontal | Primary, marketing | 120px width |
| Stacked | Social, square formats | 80px width |
| Symbol Only | Favicon, app icon, small | 32px width |
| Wordmark Only | Text-heavy contexts | 100px width |

**Clear Space:**
Minimum clear space equals the height of the "O" in ROANOKE on all sides.

**Incorrect Usage:**
- Never stretch or distort
- Never apply effects (shadows, glows, bevels)
- Never place on busy backgrounds without container
- Never alter colors outside approved palette
- Never rearrange elements
- Never add additional text to logo lockup

### 2.3 Typography

**Primary Typeface: Custom "Roanoke Sans"**
- Commissioned exclusive typeface
- Geometric base with organic terminals
- Inspired by colonial-era signage with modern refinement
- Weights: Light, Regular, Medium, Bold, Black

**Secondary Typeface: "Roanoke Serif"**
- For long-form content, lore, official documents
- Classical proportions with subtle weathering
- Weights: Regular, Medium, Bold

**Tertiary Typeface: "Roanoke Mono"**
- Technical documentation, code, UI elements
- Based on period typewriter aesthetics
- Weights: Regular, Bold

**Digital Fallbacks:**
- Sans: Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Serif: Libre Baskerville, Georgia, serif
- Mono: JetBrains Mono, Consolas, monospace

**Type Scale:**

| Level | Size | Weight | Line Height | Use |
|-------|------|--------|-------------|-----|
| Display 1 | 72px | Black | 1.1 | Hero headlines |
| Display 2 | 56px | Bold | 1.15 | Section headers |
| H1 | 40px | Bold | 1.2 | Page titles |
| H2 | 32px | Bold | 1.25 | Section titles |
| H3 | 24px | Medium | 1.3 | Subsections |
| H4 | 20px | Medium | 1.35 | Card titles |
| Body Large | 18px | Regular | 1.6 | Lead paragraphs |
| Body | 16px | Regular | 1.6 | Standard text |
| Body Small | 14px | Regular | 1.5 | Secondary text |
| Caption | 12px | Regular | 1.4 | Labels, metadata |

### 2.4 Iconography

**Icon Style:**
- 2px stroke weight at 24px size
- Rounded caps and joins
- 24px grid base, scalable to 16px, 32px, 48px
- Organic, slightly imperfect feel (not mechanical precision)

**Icon Categories:**
| Category | Count | Examples |
|----------|-------|----------|
| Navigation | 24 | Menu, back, home, search, settings |
| Actions | 36 | Build, craft, attack, defend, trade |
| Items | 100+ | Weapons, tools, resources, food |
| Status | 18 | Health, hunger, stamina, temperature |
| Social | 16 | Friends, guild, chat, emotes |
| System | 20 | Save, load, exit, volume, display |

### 2.5 Photography & Imagery

**Photography Style:**
- Naturalistic, never over-processed
- Golden hour and blue hour preference
- Human subjects show resilience, determination
- Environments emphasize scale and mystery
- Never staged or stock-feeling

**In-Game Capture Guidelines:**
- 4K minimum resolution
- HDR when available
- UI disabled for marketing shots
- Cinematic camera angles
- Weather and lighting optimized per shot

**Illustration Style:**
- Painterly, traditional media aesthetic
- Visible brushwork and texture
- Muted, period-appropriate palettes
- Reminiscent of John White colonial illustrations
- Never cartoonish or overly stylized

### 2.6 Motion & Animation

**Motion Principles:**
| Principle | Description | Timing |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Organic | Natural easing, never mechanical | ease-in-out-cubic |
| Purposeful | Motion serves function, never decorative | 200-400ms standard |
| Subtle | Enhance, never distract | Minimal travel distance |
| Consistent | Same interactions, same motions | Documented library |

**Animation Contexts:**
- UI transitions: 200ms ease-out
- Page transitions: 400ms ease-in-out
- Loading states: Infinite subtle pulse
- Celebrations: 600ms with overshoot
- Errors: 300ms shake, 2 cycles

## 3. Verbal Identity

### 3.1 Voice & Tone

**Brand Voice (Constant):**
> Knowledgeable guide who has seen much but reveals little, inviting you to discover rather than being told.

**Tone Variations by Context:**

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Marketing | Mysterious, inviting | "Something waits in the mist. Will you find it?" |
| Onboarding | Warm, encouraging | "Your journey begins here. Take your time." |
| Achievement | Proud, understated | "You've done what few have. The land remembers." |
| Error/Failure | Empathetic, motivating | "The wilderness is unforgiving. Rise again." |
| Community | Familiar, inclusive | "We've all been there. Here's what works." |
| Corporate | Professional, confident | "Roanoke represents the future of interactive entertainment." |
| Support | Patient, solution-focused | "Let's solve this together. First, try..." |

### 3.2 Messaging Framework

**Tagline:**
> "What happened at Roanoke?"

**Positioning Statement:**
> For players who seek more than entertainment, Roanoke is the survival experience that combines historical mystery with emergent community, offering a world where every choice matters and every player leaves a legacy.

**Elevator Pitches:**

*10-Second:*
> "Roanoke is where the lost colony mystery becomes your survival story. Build, explore, discover—and maybe uncover what really happened."

*30-Second:*
> "In 1590, 117 colonists vanished from Roanoke Island. The only clue: 'CROATOAN' carved in a tree. Our game drops you into that mystery—not as a historian, but as a survivor. Build your settlement, uncover the truth, and become part of a community that's writing new history every day."

*60-Second:*
> "Roanoke reimagines America's oldest mystery as an immersive survival experience. You'll build shelters, hunt for food, craft tools, and explore a procedurally-generated wilderness inspired by the real Outer Banks. But Roanoke is more than survival—it's discovery. Piece together what happened to the lost colonists through environmental storytelling, hidden lore, and secrets that take the community months to uncover. Join millions of players who aren't just playing a game—they're building a world. That's why they say: 'Are y'all getting on Roanoke tonight?'"

### 3.3 Key Messages by Audience

**Gamers:**
- "The survival game that respects your intelligence"
- "Deep enough to lose yourself in, fair enough to master"
- "Where every player's story becomes legend"

**Press/Media:**
- "The most ambitious indie game becomes the platform that challenges Steam"
- "How a two-person team built a billion-dollar universe"
- "The game that proved community-first development works"

**Investors:**
- "70% year-over-year growth in a recession-resistant category"
- "Platform economics with 85% gross margins at scale"
- "The infrastructure layer for the next generation of entertainment"

**Partners:**
- "Access 50M+ highly engaged users with premium demographics"
- "Integration that enhances rather than interrupts gameplay"
- "Co-creation opportunities with an authentic community"

**Developers:**
- "The engine that makes procedural generation accessible"
- "5% revenue share—because your success is our success"
- "Join the ecosystem that's reshaping game development"

### 3.4 Terminology Guide

**Use / Don't Use:**

| Use | Don't Use | Reason |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| Player | User, Customer | Emphasizes experience over transaction |
| World | Game, App | Roanoke is a place, not a product |
| Discover | Unlock, Get | Emphasizes exploration over acquisition |
| The Colony | The Community | In-lore reference to player base |
| Settlement | Base, House | Period-appropriate terminology |
| Craft | Make, Build (for items) | Establishes unique verb |
| Perish | Die, Get killed | Maintains tone, reduces violence framing |
| Legacy | Achievement, Score | Emphasizes permanence |

**Lore-Specific Terminology:**

| Term | Definition | Public Usage |
|------|------------|--------------|
| Croatoan | The mystery, the unknown | Yes |
| The Vanishing | Original colony disappearance | Yes |
| Whispers | Hints/clues to lore | Community only |
| The Knowing | Full lore understanding | Earned, never explained |
| Founders | Earliest players | With reverence |

## 4. Application Guidelines

### 4.1 Digital Applications

**Website:**
- Dark theme primary (Croatoan Black base)
- Light theme available (Colony White base)
- Full-bleed imagery with text overlay
- Minimal chrome, maximum immersion
- Persistent subtle ambient audio (optional)

**Social Media:**
- Profile images: Symbol logo on Forest Deep
- Cover images: Rotating in-game vistas
- Post format: Single focus, minimal text overlay
- Stories/Reels: Raw gameplay, minimal editing

**Email:**
- Clean, single-column layout
- Dark theme preferred
- Personal tone (from specific team members)
- Minimal images, fast loading

**App/Game UI:**
- Diegetic where possible (UI exists in-world)
- Non-diegetic UI: Minimal, edge-positioned
- No HUD option available
- Accessibility options comprehensive

### 4.2 Print Applications

**Business Cards:**
- Black stock, soft-touch matte
- Gold foil logo
- Minimal information
- QR code to personalized landing

**Stationery:**
- Premium uncoated stock
- Letterpress logo
- Generous margins
- Handwritten elements encouraged

**Packaging:**
- Sustainable materials exclusively
- Unboxing experience designed
- Collectible quality
- Minimal plastic/none

### 4.3 Environmental Applications

**Trade Show Booths:**
- Immersive, walk-through experience
- Practical effects (fog, lighting, sound)
- Staff in period-inspired (not costume) attire
- Demo stations with privacy

**Retail Installations:**
- Modular, scalable system
- Self-contained demo units
- Ambient audio/visual loop
- Minimal staff requirement

**Office/HQ:**
- Workspace reflects brand (not theme park)
- Subtle environmental storytelling
- Functional first, aesthetic second
- Community artifacts displayed

### 4.4 Merchandise Guidelines

**Apparel:**
- Subtle branding preferred
- Inside jokes/references for fans
- Quality basics (no cheap promo feel)
- Sustainable materials

**Collectibles:**
- Museum-quality aspirational
- Limited runs with authentication
- Functional where possible
- Investment-grade at premium tier

**Partnerships:**
- Partner brand secondary to Roanoke
- Authentic integration (no forced mashups)
- Limited editions only (no mass-market dilution)
- Approval process required

## 5. Brand Governance

| Asset Type | Approval Required From | Turnaround |
|------------|------------------------|------------|

| External marketing | Brand Director | 48 hours |

## 6. Legal Protections

### 6.1 Trademarks

**Registered Marks:**
- ROANOKE (word mark) - Class 9, 41
- Croatoan Symbol (design mark) - Class 9, 41
- "What happened at Roanoke?" (tagline) - Class 41
- Roanoke Engine (word mark) - Class 9, 42

**Trademark Usage:**
- First use in document: ROANOKE™
- Subsequent uses: Roanoke (capitalized)
- Always as adjective: "Roanoke game" not "play Roanoke"

### 6.2 Copyright

**Copyright Notice:**
> © 2025 Roanoke Interactive, Inc. All rights reserved.

**User-Generated Content:**
- Players retain copyright on original creations
- License granted to Roanoke for promotion
- Community guidelines govern usage

### 6.3 Brand Protection

**Monitoring:**
- Trademark watch service active
- Social listening for misuse
- Counterfeit detection program
- Domain monitoring

**Enforcement:**
- Graduated response (education first)
- Cease and desist for commercial misuse
- Legal action for counterfeits
- Community self-policing encouraged
